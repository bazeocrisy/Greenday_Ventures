The Owner's Decision

Selling a business is not only a financial decision.

It can affect employees, customers, family members, identity, and the future of everything the owner built. Greenday Venture approaches those conversations with patience, direct communication, and respect for the people involved.

Protect what carries value

The reputation, relationships, operating knowledge and local identity of a business may matter as much as its financial performance.

Plan around people

Employees, customers, family members and the owner all experience a transition differently. Those realities belong in the conversation early.

Build the right transition

No single structure fits every owner. Timing, involvement, continuity and future leadership should be discussed openly.

Our Approach

Four commitments that shape every conversation.

Legacy and continuity

Focused on preserving what already works. A company's name, standards, and reputation are part of its value, not details to be reorganized away.

Long-term ownership

Focused on long-term ownership — holding and operating a business over a long horizon rather than preparing it for a quick resale.

Respect for employees and customers

Committed to understanding how the crew works and what customers expect before proposing any change.

Confidential, direct communication

Approached with discretion. Discussions are intended to remain private, questions get straight answers, and there is no pressure to continue.

How It Starts

A clear beginning to a confidential process.

  1. Private introduction

    You reach out, or an advisor introduces us. Your interest is handled with care from the first message.

  2. Mutual-fit conversation

    We talk about the company, your goals, and your timing. You decide how much to share and when.

  3. Thoughtful evaluation

    If both sides see a fit, we move at a pace that keeps the business steady and the discussion discreet.
