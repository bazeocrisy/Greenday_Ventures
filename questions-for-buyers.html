For Owners

Questions worth asking any buyer.

The right buyer should be willing to answer difficult questions before asking an owner to make a life-changing decision. These are worth asking of anyone who approaches you, including Greenday Venture.

Owners of well-run trade businesses get approached constantly. Most of those approaches lead nowhere, and a few lead somewhere an owner later regrets. The difference usually comes down to what was asked at the beginning.

Take this list to any conversation. Write the answers down. A buyer who is comfortable with careful questions is telling you something useful, and so is one who is not.

0 of 28 marked

Tick the questions you have asked and note the answers as you go. Everything you type stays in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

01

Ownership Intentions

Start here. Everything else follows from what the buyer actually intends to do with the company.

02

Employees and Culture

Your team will feel the transition before your customers do.

03

Business Identity

The name, the reputation and the way the work gets done are assets, not details.

04

Seller Involvement

Be specific about this early. Vague answers here cause the most friction later.

05

Financial and Transaction Structure

Ask these before you are emotionally committed to a deal.

06

Process and Confidentiality

Your information is valuable whether or not a transaction happens.

07

References and Experience

A buyer's history, including the parts that did not go well, tells you more than their pitch.

One Last Thing

A trustworthy buyer is not threatened by careful questions.

If asking these makes a conversation uncomfortable, that is information worth having early rather than late.
