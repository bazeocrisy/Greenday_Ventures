For Professionals

For brokers and professional advisors.

Greenday Venture welcomes professionally represented opportunities that fit its home-service focus, and works to make the process straightforward for the advisor as well as the owner.

Who this page is for

Business brokers, M&A advisors, CPAs, attorneys, wealth advisors and other professionals who represent owners of established home-service companies.

What fits

  • Plumbing, HVAC, electrical, landscaping, restoration and other specialty trades.
  • Metro Atlanta and the Southeast.
  • Established operating businesses with a real history, repeat customers and an experienced team.
  • Owners considering retirement, succession or a staged step back, whether the timeline is this year or several years out.

Financial parameters are handled in conversation rather than published here, because fixed thresholds on a web page tend to screen out good opportunities before anyone has looked at them properly. Ask directly and you will get a direct answer.

How Greenday Venture works with advisors

  • Representation is respected. There is no interest in going around an advisor to reach their client, before, during or after a discussion.
  • Replies are direct. You should get a clear yes, no or specific question rather than silence. If something is not a fit, you will be told why.
  • Confidentiality is taken seriously. Information shared in a review is not circulated, and formal exchanges should be governed by an appropriate agreement.
  • Questions come early. Where there is a concern about fit, it is raised at the start rather than after weeks of process.

What helps a preliminary review

An accurate, organised summary makes a fast answer possible. Useful detail includes services and service area, years in operation, headcount and role mix, customer concentration, owner involvement, the reason for the transition, the owner's preferred timing, and the general shape of financial performance.

Incomplete information is not a barrier to an introduction, but it does slow the answer down.

Financing and structure

Financing capability and transaction structure can be discussed directly when an opportunity appears aligned with Greenday Venture's acquisition focus. Those conversations are held with the advisor rather than conducted through a website, and questions about them are welcome at whatever point in the process you consider appropriate.
